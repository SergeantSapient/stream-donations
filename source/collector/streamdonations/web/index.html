      YouTube

      Public live chat, or your own Super Chat history

      Any live stream. Super Chats, stickers, and memberships come from the public chat. You do not need to own the channel or sign in with Google.

      Google sign-in is optional. It only reads your own channel’s Super Chats from the last 30 days. YouTube’s default Data API quota is 10,000 units a day, each history or broadcast check costs 1 unit, and the day resets at midnight Pacific Time. The shortest interval is 5 minutes. If the quota is used up, signed-in calls wait for that reset. Public live chat does not spend this quota. While the Google app is in Testing, the Gmail address you use must be listed under Google Auth Platform → Audience → Test users, or Google shows “Access blocked”.

      “The user is not enabled for live streaming” means that Google account cannot go live. Memberships on your own broadcast are skipped. A public video URL still works, and you do not need to enable live streaming to watch someone else’s chat.

      Ledger

      Used when a membership or subscription arrives without a price. YouTube counts “Sent 5 … gift memberships” as five, then multiplies by this price. Kick bundles do the same. A Rumble bundle is this price for the first subscription and $5.00 for each additional one, so five Rumble subs are $24.99 when this price is $4.99. Leave it blank and those amounts stay empty.

      Up to five goals. The chat line names the next one that still has a name, and how many dollars are left. A cent short of a goal still counts as reached. Turn tiers off when this stream has none.

      Kick coins convert at 100 coins = $1. Under $5 is a Super Sticker. $5 and above is a Super Chat. Export times use this computer’s time zone. The CSV columns are Date, Time, Donor, Amount, Platform, Type, Currency, Amount USD, and Message.
